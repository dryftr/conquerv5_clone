# Screenshot Checklist — Conquer Reborn

Quick reference for capturing visual assets during development. Grab these as you go — you'll thank yourself later.

## World & Environment
- [ ] Individual tile types (terrain, water, mountains, forests, etc.)
- [ ] Tile transitions and blending (where biomes meet)
- [ ] Weather effects or atmospheric overlays
- [ ] Minimap views
- [ ] Fog of war states (revealed vs unrevealed)

## UI & Gameplay
- [ ] Main menu / title screen
- [ ] Game setup screen (nation selection, map options)
- [ ] Turn overview / status panel
- [ ] Diplomacy screen
- [ ] Economy / resource management
- [ ] Military view (unit selection, movement, combat)
- [ ] AI nation decision moments (observation mode)
- [ ] End-of-turn summary

## Progression & Milestones
- [ ] Early game (starting position, first moves)
- [ ] Mid-game (expanded territory, diplomatic states)
- [ ] Late-game (domination, victory conditions)
- [ ] Victory / defeat screens

## Character & Personality
- [ ] AI nation personality moments (unique behaviors, dialogue)
- [ ] Observation mode highlights
- [ ] "Honest AI" transparency moments (AI reasoning visible to player)

## Key Art & Press
- [ ] Logo variations (light, dark, icon-only)
- [ ] Title treatment on dark background
- [ ] Signature gameplay screenshot (THE one that goes everywhere)
- [ ] Side-by-side: original Conquer vs Reborn (when ready)

## How to Capture
- **Linux:** `Shift+PrtSc` for area select, `PrtSc` for full screen, or use Flameshot for annotations
- **Save to:** `marketing/screenshots/` with descriptive filenames (e.g., `world-tiles-forest-transition.png`)
- **Reference art:** Drop into `marketing/key-art/references/`

---

*The best screenshots happen in the moment. Don't wait for "finished" — capture the journey.*
